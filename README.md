🛒 Market Condition — বাজার দর
Know Today's Market Prices. Shop Smarter.

Market Condition is a modern, responsive web application designed to help people in Bangladesh explore the prices of everyday essential commodities in one place. It provides an easy way to view current listed prices, compare them with previous prices, identify price increases and decreases, and explore detailed product information. 

🌟 Overview

Finding and comparing the prices of everyday essentials can be challenging. Market Condition aims to make this process simpler by bringing essential commodity price information together in a single, user-friendly platform.

The application presents product prices in Bangla, making the information accessible to Bengali-speaking users. Users can explore different product categories, see price movements, compare today's prices with previous prices, and navigate to individual product detail pages.

The project focuses on simplicity, accessibility, responsive design, and a convenient browsing experience.

✨ Key Features

📊 Market Price Overview — Explore listed prices of essential commodities from a single dashboard.

📈 Price Movement Tracking — Identify products whose prices have increased or decreased.

🔄 Price Comparison — Compare today's prices with previous prices and view available historical comparisons.

🛍️ Product Catalog — Browse a collection of everyday essential products.

🗂️ Category-Based Browsing — Explore products by their respective categories.

🔎 Product Details — Open individual product pages to view additional price information.

📢 Live-Style Price Marquee — Browse a scrolling summary of product prices and price changes.

🔐 User Authentication — Access the sign-in and sign-up functionality.

👤 Profile Management — Update profile information where supported by the configured authentication features.

📱 Responsive Interface — Designed to provide a convenient experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

🇧🇩 Bangla-Friendly UI — Displays product information and prices in Bengali for local users.

🚀 Live Deployment — Hosted on Vercel for public access.
